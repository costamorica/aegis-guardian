# Aegis Guardian Manifesto

Most monitoring tools are good at reporting that something is broken.

Fewer tools explain why.

Even fewer can recover safely without becoming an additional source of risk.

Aegis Guardian exists to support the administrator, not replace them. It handles repetitive observation, evidence collection and carefully bounded recovery actions while leaving important decisions to a human operator.

## The Aegis Method

### 1. Observe

Never assume the state of a system. Measure it.

### 2. Understand

A failed check must include evidence. A status without an explanation is noise.

### 3. Classify

Not every failure is equally urgent. Guardian distinguishes informational findings, warnings, recoverable incidents and critical failures.

### 4. Repair carefully

A recovery action must be known, documented, allow-listed and reversible whenever possible.

### 5. Record everything

Every check, decision, attempted repair and outcome must be traceable.

### 6. Inform intelligently

Guardian should reduce alert fatigue. A transient issue repaired safely does not require the same response as a persistent outage.

### 7. Keep humans in control

Guardian may recommend. Guardian may execute pre-approved actions. Guardian must never silently redesign the host it protects.
